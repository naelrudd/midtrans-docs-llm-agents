# What if I don't have an ID/KTP?

Indonesian Identity Card (KTP) is one of the documents required for Indonesian citizens (WNI) to activate Midtrans payment service.

If you don't have a KTP, you can apply for one to the Civil Registry Service Office (Disdukcapil) or your local Ward Office (Kantor Kelurahan).

While waiting for your account activation, you can explore various features and make transaction simulations transactions through the **Sandbox** mode on your dashboard.

Detailed information regarding the documents required for account activation can be accessed through [this page ↗.](https://midtrans.com/passport)

All requested documents must be submitted as a condition for requesting your account activation.
