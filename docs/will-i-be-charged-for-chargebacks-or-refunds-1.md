# Will I be charged for refunds for GoPay?

No. Midtrans does not charge additional fees for the refund process. **The customer will receive a 100% refund** and Merchants **will not be charged** fees for transactions that have been successful(Settlement).

**Example:**

The Midtrans fee for transactions that have been successful (Settlement) using GoPay is 2% per transaction + VAT (11%).

If the product price is **Rp. 100,000** and the customer pays using GoPay, where the Midtrans fees is **2% per transaction = Rp. 2,000**, then VAT (11%) is taken from that value, bringing the total fees to **Rp. 2,220**.

Then the customer will receive a 100% refund with a value of Rp. 100,000

> ❗️ IMPORTANT ❗
>
> Transactions that you want to [refund ↗](https://docs.midtrans.com/docs/what-payment-method-that-have-refund-feature) must have a **SETTLEMENT** status on your Midtrans account (Transaction menu).
