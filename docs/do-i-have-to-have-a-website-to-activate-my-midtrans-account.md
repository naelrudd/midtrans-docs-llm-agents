# Do I have to have a website to activate my Midtrans account?

You can still apply for account activation by using other links(s) that represent your business, such as your business's social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram), or by attaching a product catalog.

The following are examples of NON-website or application URLs that we can accept and process for account activation:

* Social Media: <https://www.instagram.com/padmala.atelier/>
* Market Place: <https://www.tokopedia.com/kertasive>

If you register your business using the product catalog, your business can only accept payments via Bank Transfer (Permata bank VA, BNI VA, BRI VA, & Mandiri Bill), QRIS, and GoPay.
