# What is the applicable transaction fee for QRIS?

MDR or fee set by Bank Indonesia for regular type merchants is **0.7% per successful transaction**.

Or you can contact our team via [Contact Us ↗](https://midtrans.com/contact-us/informasi-transaksi/berapa-biaya-penggunaan-layanan-midtrans) page, fill the form and submit it if you need confirmation regarding MDR or fees for special types of merchants.
