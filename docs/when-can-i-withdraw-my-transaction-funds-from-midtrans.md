# When can I withdraw my transaction funds from Midtrans?

For payment methods that the transaction funds go through Midtrans, you can request a payout from the [billing menu ↗](https://dashboard.midtrans.com/wallstreet_billings) inside the Merchant Administration Portal (MAP). You can request a payout every business day, but please note the transaction only be requested for a payout at least if it has success (settlement) in **3 business days**. This is the cut-off time that has been determined by the bank, to ensure that the funds paid by the customer have been successfully on the bank's side.

> 🚧 Note💡
>
> To withdraw your money for the first time, please pay attention to this information:\
> You have submitted all required registration data/documents and at least have 1 success/settlement transaction, so within 3 days you can do the withdrawal process.
>
> If the data/documents are incomplete during the registration process, Midtrans's team would contact you through email and Whatsapp within 3 working days after you've finished the registration process.
