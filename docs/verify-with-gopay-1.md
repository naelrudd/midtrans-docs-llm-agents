# Verify with GoPay

A flow where users verify their identity using their existing GoPay account.

## Verify with GoPay

Verify with GoPay enables you to confirm a user’s identity (e.g. KTP) through their GoPay account.

* **GoPay Plus**: For users who are already verified in GoPay, verification is completed quickly through only face verification.

![](https://files.readme.io/a70c2a20e3c6889dd714d532849e58f695f511edcbdb25e437f56d88d9432870-My_Jam_Frame.png)

<br />

* **Not GoPay Plus**: For users who are not yet verified, GoPay guides them through ID (KTP) capture and selfie submission within a seamless flow powered by GoPay’s 0-click technology and security checks such as ID anti-tampering detection, liveness verification, and deepfake prevention.

![](https://files.readme.io/d3d6badeb6aea954510fc294fb70fec6ad40267ecb052e966bfd6e5ca7f6d773-My_Jam_Frame_1.png)

<br />

## Login with GoPay as Pre-requisite

In order to use Verify with GoPay, first users need to be account linked. If not linked (linking token is not presented), Login with GoPay will be invoked automatically. Then, the Verify with GoPay flow will be invoked.
