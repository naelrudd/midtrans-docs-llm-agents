# What are the website or application criterias for registering a Midtrans account?

Here are the criteria for a website or app to qualify:

1. Your business website or application must be ready, active, accessible, and match with the business category you’ve registered on Midtrans.
2. Make sure the information on the products/services sold on the website or application is clear and complete.
3. Make sure you’re showing the price in IDR or Rupiah on the website or application that you’ll register.
4. Alternatively, you can provide a link that represents your business (your business's website, marketplace, or social media).
5. If you run your business through an app, please include your App Store or Play Store URL link.
