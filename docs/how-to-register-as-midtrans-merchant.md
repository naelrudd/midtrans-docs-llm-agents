# How to register as Midtrans merchant?

You can register as a Midtrans merchant by following the steps below:\ <br />

1. Access Midtrans' registration page at this link: [Registration Page ↗](https://midtrans.com/passport).
2. Click the "**Register Now**" button and you will be redirected to the registration data filling page as an attachment below.

   <Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/1e3f2f8-mceclip1_1.png" />
3. Fill in the fields: **Business Name, Full Name, Business Email, Business Phone Number, Password, and Password confirmation**.
   > 🚧 Notes 📝
   >
   > Please make sure the Business Email and Business Phone Number haven't registered in the other Midtrans/GoBiz/GoFood account yet.
4. Click "**Register**" then check the email inbox that you've registered in step 3. Click **Verify and complete registration** to confirm your registration process. Example in attachment below.

   <Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/27c8cca-Register_2.PNG" />
5. You will be redirected to Midtrans' login page. Now you can log in using the email and password that has been set in step 3.
6. Your account has been registered in Midtrans' system. To activate the account you have to finish the passport process.

> 🚧 Notes 📝
>
> Note: no minimum/maximum transaction limit must be fulfilled by merchants if they want to join. Midtrans and make sure the data/documents you provide are fully complete so we can proceed immediately and you can withdraw money.

> 📘 Info :bulb:
>
> Ensure your website/application/social media URL can be accessed by the public and there's the product with the price is visible.
