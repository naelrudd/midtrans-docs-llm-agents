# Are we completely protected from fraudulent transactions?

While our fraud detection system is exceptionally effective at preventing online frauds, we cannot guarantee that fraud attempts can be completely isolated.

The ever-evolving nature of fraud means it is an inevitable risk online merchants need to embrace and adapt to, just like how businesses conventionally deal with bad receivables. Should we ever notice unusually high dispute rate or fraudulent attempts on your account, we will proactively reach out to see how we can help.
