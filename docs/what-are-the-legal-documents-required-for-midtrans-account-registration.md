# What are the legal documents required for Midtrans account registration?

The legal documents required to register an account on Midtrans will vary depending on your type of business.

Here’s the guideline for required documents based on your business type:

# For Domestic Business Entity (Local Business)

|                                                                            |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Individual                                                                 | <ul><li>Owner’s ID (KTP)</li></ul><ul><li>NPWP</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Business Entity (PT, CV, PMA)                                              | <ul><li>Latest Company Deed</li><li>Decree of Ministry of Justice and Human Rights on Deed of Amendment / Establishment</li><li>Director's ID (KTP) or passport</li><li>Director's Tax ID (NPWP)</li><li>Company Tax ID (NPWP)</li><li>NIB/SIUP/TDP</li><li>Other business licenses according to business activities</li></ul>            |
| Foundation (Education, Health Services, Religious Activities, Social Fund) | <ul><li>Latest Company Deed</li><li>Decree of Ministry of Justice and Human Rights on Deed of Amendment/Establishment</li><li>Chairman's ID (KTP) or Passport</li><li>Chairman's Tax ID (NPWP)</li><li>Foundation Tax ID (NPWP)</li><li>NIB/SIUP/TDP</li><li>Other foundation licenses according to the foundation’s activities</li></ul> |

<br />

# For Foreign Business Entity

|                 |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :-------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Individual      | <ul><li>Owner’s passport</li><li>Owner’s KITAS/KITAP/KIMS</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Business Entity | <ul><li>Owner’s/Director’s passport</li><li>Owner’s/Director's Tax ID (NPWP)</li><li>Front page of bank book/screenshot of company bank mutation</li><li>Company Registration Documents</li><li>Company Deed Number</li><li>Deed of Incorporation</li><li>Investor Information Documents</li><li>Business License Document (Issued by country of origin)</li><li>Other business licenses according to business activities</li></ul> |

<br />

The documents need to be uploaded to activate your Midtrans account by clicking the banner "**One more step! Complete your registration process here**" located on the top of your Midtrans dashboard.
