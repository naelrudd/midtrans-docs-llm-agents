# How do I view and manage my transactions on Midtrans?

Midtrans provides all merchants with a [Merchant Administration Portal (MAP) ↗](https://dashboard.midtrans.com/login?language=en) dashboard.

In MAP, you will be able to:

* View and search transactions (and their details);
* Manage transactions (i.e. cancel transactions);
* Download transactions report;
* Set payments configuration;
* View your Access Keys;
* Set email and https notifications;
* Check how much money your business receive;
* Check how much money you will receive;
* Request to withdraw your money;
* etc.
