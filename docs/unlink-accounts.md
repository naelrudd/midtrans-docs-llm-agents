# Unlink Accounts

How to perform or receive unlinking account events.

# Scenarios of Unlinking

For any of your users who have been linked with GoPay it can be unlinked by:

1. You, providing a way on your app to unlink from GoPay, or
2. GoPay, we have a menu to unlink users' account with any linked merchants.

# Documentations

There are two ways of unlinking:

1. Manually provides a way to unlink on your app - [Unbind API](https://docs.midtrans.com/reference/unbind-api)
2. Listen to unlinking notifications - [Linking & Unlinking Notification](https://docs.midtrans.com/reference/account-linking-unlinking-notification)
