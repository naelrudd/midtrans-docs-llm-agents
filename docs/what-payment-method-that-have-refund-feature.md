> ❗️ IMPORTANT ❗
>
> * If there are **any unexpected behaviors** related to the refund process, please contact our support team through [this link ↗](https://midtrans.com/contact-us/transaction-information/prosedur-refund-pengembalian-dana).
> * Please note that the **SLA (Service Level Agreement) is a working day**, funds may be returned sooner or later than the information stated.
> * For the **payment method that doesn't have the refund feature**, or **for transactions that have exceeded the refund period limit** in Midtrans, the refund process can be done manually from merchant to customer.
> * The maximum refund period follows the standards set by the acquiring bank and payment provider. This may vary depending on internal policies, account level, and security procedures of each provider. In addition, Midtrans may impose additional limitations to ensure transaction security and risk mitigation.
