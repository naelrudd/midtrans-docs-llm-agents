# Can I know the reasons why a transaction is DENIED?

Yes. Our merchants can access our Merchant Administration Portal (MAP) and see the [Transaction Page ↗](https://dashboard.midtrans.com/payments) for more details of a particular transaction, including its status.

[Click here ↗](https://docs.midtrans.com/docs/why-was-my-customers-transaction-rejected) to see the step by step instructions and common reasons for rejection.
