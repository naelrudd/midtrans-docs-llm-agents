# Differences in available payment methods between Individuals and Business Entities

Midtrans provides a wide selection of payment methods that you can choose according to your business needs. The differences between business entities and individuals are in the number of payment methods that can be used and the documents that need to be prepared.

![](https://files.readme.io/d2fa6f1-image.png)

Please note that **there are several payment methods that you have to activate first** after you register, either by filling out additional forms or attaching supporting documents.

You can see the requirements for adding a payment method at [this link ↗.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11-S79vAkDX97cHH6ecR-YINIeIbNn_-DaSsJ-e_4Xl4/edit)

You can see the legal documents required for Midtrans account registration at [this link ↗](https://docs.midtrans.com/docs/what-are-the-legal-documents-required-for-midtrans-account-registration).
