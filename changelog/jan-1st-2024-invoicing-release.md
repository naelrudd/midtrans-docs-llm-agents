# Invoicing Release

We're excited to announce the Beta launch of Midtrans Invoicing, a new feature designed to simplify your account receivable process.

**What is Invoicing?**

Invoicing allows you to:

* Create and send custom branded invoices: Design professional invoices with your logo and branding directly within the Midtrans Dashboard.
* Generate payment options: Offer various payment methods to your clients, including:\
  Payment Links: Create a secure payment link that your clients can easily access and use to pay online.\
  Virtual Account Numbers: Provide your clients with unique virtual account numbers for secure and trackable bank transfers.
* Automated reminders: Set up automatic reminders to nudge clients about outstanding invoices.\
  Reconcile payments automatically: Effortlessly match received payments with the corresponding invoices.

**Getting Started**

Invoicing is currently available in Beta mode and accessible through your Midtrans Dashboard under the "Invoicing" menu. No manual activation is required.

We're actively working to improve Invoicing and would love to hear your feedback and feature requests. Please share your thoughts through [our product board](/page/feature-request).
