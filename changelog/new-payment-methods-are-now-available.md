# New Payment Methods are Now Available!

We’re excited to announce that Midtrans now supports new payment methods :

1. Bank Syariah Indonesia Virtual Account (BSI VA)
2. SeaBank Virtual Account (Seabank VA) -- *\*available soon*
3. Danamon Virtual Account (Danamon VA)
4. Static QRIS

With these new options, your customers can now complete transactions through their preferred banks, giving them more convenience and helping you reach more users seamlessly.

## How to Enable it?

### If you already have MID

To enable these payment methods, simply go to your Midtrans Dashboard and open the [Add New Payment Method menu](https://dashboard.midtrans.com/new_payment_method) to register.
