# All payment method support in Promo Management

Now you can easily run self funded promotions via Promo Management for all payment methods - whereas previously only card based promotion campaign can be created. You can easily create campaign combining multiple payment methods along with other features in Promo Management e.g. SKU based promotions.

Learn more about promo management [here](/docs/promo-management).

Haven't activated promo management yet? Activate it for your business in minutes without additional sign up fee via [Dashboard > Promo](https://dashboard.midtrans.com/promo).
