# Release Note: Saved Filters Are Here!

**What’s New**\
Your export filters now work smarter. The system will remember the last filter you used, so the next time you want to export, you don’t have to start from scratch.  This applies when you choose “Add Filter” from the dropdown while logged in with the same email. If you want to go back to default, just click “Clear Filter.” Clearing will reset the view, but your last filter is still saved and ready for the next export.

**Why this matters to you:**\
Less repetitive setup, fewer mistakes, and faster access to the reports you need, so you can spend more time making decisions and running your business.

**Services with this benefit:**

Transaction page and Balance page in Midtrans Dashboard.

***

**Apa yang Baru**\
Filter ekspor Anda kini lebih pintar. Sistem akan mengingat filter terakhir yang Anda gunakan, sehingga saat ekspor berikutnya Anda tidak perlu mengatur ulang dari awal.  Fitur ini berlaku saat Anda memilih “Tambah Filter” di dropdown dengan login menggunakan email yang sama. Jika ingin kembali ke default, cukup klik “Hapus Filter.” Tampilan akan direset, tetapi filter terakhir Anda tetap tersimpan untuk ekspor berikutnya.
